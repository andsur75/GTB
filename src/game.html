How it works

  • Ringing payphones (green blips) give you Artur's missions. The last ones rob the bank.
  • Stolen cash is carried as loot: drop it at Artur's Garage or the safehouse before the police catch you.
  • Wasted: you wake up at the hospital. Busted: you leave the police station. Either way, your guns are gone.
